# Little Dori — Privacy Policy

_Last updated: August 5, 2026_

Little Dori is a private keepsake for the people you love. It is built so
that we — the developer — never see your data. This page explains exactly
what that means.

## What we collect

**Nothing.** Little Dori has no accounts, no analytics, no advertising,
no tracking, and no third-party services. The app makes no network
connections of its own; the only network activity is Apple's iCloud sync,
described below.

## Where your data lives

Everything you enter — people, dates, rhythms, plans, notes, photos —
is stored on your device. If you are signed in to iCloud, it is also
synced to the **private CloudKit database** of your own iCloud account so
your other devices stay in step. That database belongs to you and is
protected by your Apple Account; we have no ability to read it.

If you are not signed in to iCloud, everything simply stays on your
device, and the app says so in Settings.

## Contacts

Adding someone from Contacts uses Apple's system contact picker, which
runs outside the app: Little Dori receives only the single contact you
choose — a name, photo, phone number, and birthday if present — and keeps
that copy in its own store. The app never reads your address book beyond
what you hand it, never writes to Contacts, and works fully without any
Contacts permission.

## Notifications

Reminders are local notifications, scheduled entirely on your device.
Nothing about them leaves your phone.

## Deleting your data

Deleting a person in the app deletes their page, days, and history from
your device and your iCloud. Deleting the app removes the on-device
store; any synced data can be removed from iCloud in
Settings → your name → iCloud → Manage Account Storage.

## Children

Little Dori is not directed at children and collects no data from anyone.

## Changes

If this policy ever changes, the updated text will appear here with a new
date. Since the app collects nothing, we expect it to stay boring.
